# Creative Direction

## Objective

Create a globally distinctive digital experience, not a polished generic wedding website.

## Reference territories

Use references from:

- editorial fashion;
- luxury;
- architecture;
- museums and exhibitions;
- boutique hospitality;
- cinematic title design;
- premium digital storytelling.

Do not use conventional wedding references as the primary source.

## Avoid by default

- decorative envelopes as the central concept;
- petals, particles or romantic clichés;
- repetitive section cards;
- generic wedding icons;
- excessive scripts and ornamental typography;
- effects without narrative purpose;
- a long collection of unrelated modules.

## Current hypothesis to explore

`23·10·26 — A Living Invitation`

The date and a continuous line can form a recognizable visual and motion system. Validate it through concept work and prototypes rather than treating it as automatically approved.

## Experience priorities

1. A memorable opening.
2. Real photography with strong art direction.
3. A concise emotional narrative.
4. Clear event information.
5. Individual RSVP as the narrative conclusion.
6. WhatsApp concierge as assistance, not as the only database record.

Any visual decision must include mobile, performance, reduced-motion and accessibility behavior.
