# Approved Assets

## Binding rules

- Use real approved photographs as the default.
- Do not generate or publish AI images of Felipe or Camila without real-photo reference and explicit likeness approval.
- Do not publish restored, retouched, expanded or materially altered images without approval.
- Do not overwrite source originals.
- Historical civil-wedding photographs must be preserved and recoverable.
- Arboleda sections may contain only verified Arboleda photographs.
- Santuario / church sections may contain only verified photographs of the confirmed venue.
- Do not use placeholders or example guest photos in production.

## Required asset metadata

Every production asset must have:

- source;
- owner or copyright status;
- event / album;
- approval status;
- original path;
- optimized path;
- alt text;
- intended placement.

## Directory behavior

Prefer an explicit approval workflow such as:

```text
design/assets/originals/
design/assets/review/
design/assets/approved/
design/assets/rejected/
```

Do not move, delete or transform assets during the audit.
